The opportunity

Retailers own the product. Nobody owns the visit at home.

Chewy and Petco hold the pharmacy, Autoship and the customer — but care is decided in the home, and independent vets have no engine to reach it. Timing is now:

Vet supply is the limitThe U.S. will produce only ~76% of vets needed through 2032. Software, not buildings, adds capacity.
The solution

A software layer that dispatches independent vets — and feeds the retailer’s pharmacy

No inventory, no fleet, no clinics. Every visit can route a prescription or refill, with consent, to the partner.

Provider network

Independent mobile vets and groomers, onboarded neighbourhood by neighbourhood.

AI dispatch engine

Matching, routing, teletriage and forecasting turn provider hours into scalable capacity.

Home health record

Predicts what each pet needs next — exactly what powers an Autoship business.
